inverter.gds is an application-generated arrangement of the public
sky130_fd_sc_hd__inv_1 and sky130_fd_sc_hd__tapvpwrvgnd_1 cells, with body
terminals tied to rails by the actual tap geometry and independent body labels
removed. The underlying cells come from sky130_fd_sc_hd at the commit recorded
in the installed PDK nodeinfo: aed07ad6d4eff1861cb9d10f102d247231e3645c.

mosfet.gds was generated by the public installed open_pdks SKY130 Magic PCell
source at d400e26845538beaeb7cc5fdb9bfc06c30ea27cb, with nf=m=1 and viagate=0.
Application pin-purpose polygons preserve port metadata across GDS exchange.

current_mirror.gds and differential_pair.gds arrange two and three instances of
that same public NFET PCell, respectively, with application-generated contact
fanout, metal3 rails and port metadata. The public SKY130 via1/via2 helpers
provide their cut/enclosure geometry. These files retain the original public
source license and are independently checked by the unchanged local decks.

wire.gds is application-generated test geometry using the public PDK layer
mapping. Model and rule-deck files remain references inside the pinned local
PDK installation and are not embedded in these GDS/sidecar examples.

The generated arrangement, pin metadata, application code and verification
records are additions to the referenced public sources. They do not represent
foundry signoff or an endorsement by the original PDK authors.
